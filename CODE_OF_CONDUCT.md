# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of:

- age, body size or visible or invisible disability;
- ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression;
- level of experience, education or socio-economic status;
- nationality, personal appearance, race, caste or color;
- religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and respectful.
- Assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Trolling.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the project maintainers through the contact
listed on the repository's profile, or through GitHub's
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy.

Maintainers may remove contributions or temporarily or permanently ban
contributors whose behavior violates this code.

The full text, including the enforcement guidelines, is at the link above and
applies to this project.
